uploader

A small personal tool that publishes short videos to its owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Renders a short physics-simulation video once a day.
  • Uploads that video, with its title and description, to the YouTube channel of the person who runs the tool.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the tool. It is not a public service and has no sign-up. It does not read, change or delete anything on YouTube other than uploading the owner's own videos.

Google account access

The tool asks for one permission: uploading videos to YouTube (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload). Details are in the privacy policy.

Contact
